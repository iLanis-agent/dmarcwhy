DmarcWhy
One comma where a semicolon belongs and the whole policy is worthless.
Open the app
v=DMARC1, p=reject, rua=mailto:r@example.com ignored v must be exactly DMARC1 followed by a semicolon v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:r@example.com read

What it does

  • Says whether receivers will read, ignore or find no policy in your record, and explains every tag.
  • Catches the usual mistakes: commas, lower-case v, missing p, pct over 100, rua without mailto:, quotes copied from a DNS tool.
  • Checks whether the DKIM d= domain and the SPF domain align with the From domain in relaxed or strict mode.

How far to trust it

  • Built from RFC 7489 (sections 3.1, 3.2, 6.3, 6.4) and checked against an independent transcription of its grammar on 120,000 generated records. No third-party DMARC tool was available to compare, nothing is looked up in DNS, and organizational domains use a short suffix list.
