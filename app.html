DmarcWhy

Paste the TXT record from _dmarc.yourdomain.com. See whether receivers will read it, what each tag means, and whether a message's DKIM and SPF domains align with the From domain.

Alignment check (optional)

Rules come from RFC 7489 sections 3.1, 3.2, 6.3 and 6.4 (fetched; only the first part of the RFC was retrieved, so policy discovery, report addresses at other domains and the examples in the appendices were not read). The checker agrees with an independent transcription of the RFC grammar on 120,000 generated and mutated records. No third-party DMARC tool was available to test against. Organizational domains use a built-in list of 24 common two-part suffixes, not the full public suffix list. It does not look anything up in DNS. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
